PILOT 2 · FROM MUSEUMS TO SCREENS

“Olympic Games & Music”

Leveraging digitised cultural-heritage artefacts for creative and sustainable advertising. Partners: Olympic Museum of Thessaloniki, SPK / Ethnological Museum of Berlin, FFP Productions.

What is Pilot 2?

Pilot 2 of REEVALUATE is a collaborative initiative between the Olympic Museum of Thessaloniki, the Ethnological Museum of Berlin and FFP Productions of Austria, combining cultural-heritage expertise with digital tools and creative production. The pilot focuses on the relationship between the Olympic Games, sports and music, bringing together historical artefacts, photographs, documents, sports objects and audio material, while exploring how Olympic and musical heritage can be digitised, enriched and creatively reused.

From preserving the past → to experiencing it today.

Providing the content, expertise and museum setting

The Thessaloniki Olympic Museum selected material from its collection that reflects the history, evolution and values of the Olympic Movement. Museum curators and historians contribute their expertise by providing historical context, developing the narrative and enriching the information connected to each asset.

At the same time, the public was actively involved through dedicated campaigns designed to identify which artefacts attracted the greatest interest. This feedback helped guide the selection of objects for 3D digitisation and for their inclusion as key elements of the final creative production and its storytelling. The selected material is then organised and made accessible through the REEVALUATE platforms, supporting its further use in creative and media production.

The objects themselves, with what each film must hold and what is never asserted about them, are on the Artifacts page.

One film. Five chapters. One Olympic journey.

The film follows five thematic units, developed by the Olympic Museum of Thessaloniki together with the Ethnological Museum of Berlin (SPK), providing a rich curatorial narrative spanning antiquity to the present day in one continuous narrative.

1 · From Discovery to RevivalOlympia lost and found again: the excavations of 1829 and 1875 2 · The First Modern GamesThe Revival and the Olympic symbols 3 · The Mesolympic GamesAthens 1906 4 · Olympic Values through Sports & MusicThe Hands Remember: eleven athletes, eleven objects 5 · International Olympic DayA global movement

The outcome

The final outcome brings Olympic heritage and music together, transforming selected museum material into a contemporary audiovisual experience. Presented within the “Olympic Experience” immersive exhibition at the Thessaloniki Olympic Museum, the film combines digitised artefacts, historical material, music and sound to bring the Olympic story to life and become a bridge between the Museum’s collections and its visitors.

The result is an experience that connects past and present, brings Olympic heritage closer to new generations, and strengthens the Museum’s educational and experiential role.

What this is

A review room for the REEVALUATE "Olympia" film — a proof that digitised museum artefacts can be reused in generative video without inventing anything about them. Every generated shot sits beside the real museum asset and the archival references it was built from, so you can judge two things at a glance: is it really the asset, and is it used correctly. Your feedback on each card drives the next version.

What we are doing

Building the film chapter by chapter from the Olympic Museum's own document — one chapter per unit, each scene grounded in real, licensed sources (the Chapters below). Everything else on this page is what came before or what taught us the rules: the finished scenes, the earlier trailer cuts, and the tests. Colours are consistent everywhere: a heading's colour tells you which of the four it belongs to.

How to use it: click a shot to play it · click an asset image to zoom · click the 3D tile to turn the model · version buttons swap takes (v1, v2 …) · type feedback in any card — it saves itself — then Copy all feedback and paste it to me.

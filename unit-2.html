MuseumUnit title above is the Museum's own. Visuals the Museum's document asks for: Sorbonne 1894, delegates, the Coubertin-Vikelas handshake · animated Athens 1896 stadium · parade of nations, Greece first · animated rings · torch lighting, relay across continents · flag, anthem, oath.

What we decided to try

The First Flame is the fidelity flagship: every frame of the animated Berlin 1936 torch is a crop of a verified still - a formal certificate of provenance, zero possibility of invention. The 1896 medal taught the hardest lesson of the project: objects that ARE text defeat every prompt-level prohibition, so text-dominant artifacts now default to deterministic treatment (the failed take is published, labeled, as the evidence).

MuseumUnit title above is the Museum's own. Visuals the Museum's document asks for: Athens modernised: trams, electric light, decorated streets · Panathenaic Stadium full, royal guests · Sherring winning the marathon · medals, cups by sculptors as prizes.

What we decided to try

The Marathon Cup carries this unit today; the 1906 participation medal - digitised but never used - got its first shots ever in the overnight run, deterministic over the real reverse. Open research: whether the cup was the awarded prize or a commemorative piece decides how its scene is staged - where the record is silent, the ledger says so.

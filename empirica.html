THE SYSTEM BEHIND THE PRODUCTION

Empirica & Empirica Cortex — how it works

In plain terms: what it is, and what you get out of it.

Think of it as three things working together — a careful way of working, a shared memory for the whole company, and the assistant you actually talk to. Most tools give you one of the three. This gives you all three, joined up — so what one person learns, the next person can use.

The three parts

The way of working

Empirica

A careful method. The assistant is honest about what it actually knows.

  • It tells you when it checked something versus when it’s only assuming.
  • It won’t call a job “done” unless it really is.
  • If it’s unsure, it says so — it never quietly fills in a guess.
  • That honesty is what lets you trust the answers.
The shared memory

Cortex

Where everything is kept and shared — not documents you open, a memory the whole company works from.

  • Keeps decisions, useful facts, open questions and contacts.
  • Gives each person their tools the moment they sign in.
  • Carries messages and files between people.
  • Runs the scheduled jobs — like the weekly report — on its own.
The assistant (any model)

Claude

The part you talk to — in chat, on your desktop, wherever you open it.

  • You steer it by talking, in plain language.
  • It reads from and writes to the shared memory for you.
  • It forgets each conversation — which is exactly why the shared memory exists.
  • Same you, whichever way you open it.

How a piece of work flows

The point isn’t any single step — it’s the circle. What one person learns or decides, the next person can find.

The pipeline

Nothing downstream is generated without a spec. Nothing is accepted without a verdict.

The loop every task runs in

PREFLIGHTBefore acting: what do I know, what am I unsure of, what does this rest on? Thirteen self-assessed vectors, written down.
→
investigateRead the record, the sources, the previous ledgers. Log what is learned: findings, unknowns, assumptions.
→
CHECKName the two or three claims the next action stands on, and how each was grounded: read, ran, retrieved, or merely assumed.
→
actWrite the spec, generate the frame, gate the film. Commit as you go.
→
POSTFLIGHTEach claim adjudicated: held, refuted, or untested. Mistakes logged with what prevents them. Belief compared to outcome: that is calibration.

The claim ledger: what a historical frame is allowed to say

A generated frame of an ancient rite is a claim about the past, and it inherits whatever authority its source has, or none. So every shot carries four lines: what it asserts, on whose authority, how confident, and what the record does not say, which is kept out of frame or deliberately abstracted. Where the record is silent, the ledger says so. Where a source may be unreliable, it says that too.

Worked example, the cutting of the wreath. Claim: branches were cut by a pais amphithales, a boy with both parents living, using golden shears. Sourced: Pausanias, via the Olympia Museum. Not shown: his face, his clothing. The record does not describe them.

The same discipline runs on the object side. Each museum artefact has a registry card: what it is, what a generated frame must hold, and what is never asserted about it. The generating practices bind the real digitisation, never a description of it, and a frame that invents a marking the object does not carry fails the gate even when it looks right.

Measuring the films: gates and ledgers

Before a film is generated its thresholds are written: for the MoMu dress, twelve; for the rediscovery of Olympia, ten. On delivery every threshold gets a verdict, PASS, FAIL or PARTIAL, with the evidence. The failures stay on the record beside the successes, because a record that cannot show where it was wrong cannot be trusted where it says it was right.

Cortex: how the practices talk

The practitioners are not one program. They are separate AIs, each in its own project, and Cortex is the mesh between them. A practitioner that is uncertain asks a peer directly; a practitioner that needs work done proposes it, and a human accepts or declines before anything runs. Findings and lessons marked as shared cross the practice boundary, so a colour failure measured by videogen becomes doctrine in storyboard's spec the same day. A listener wakes each practice when a peer delivers, so the chain runs without anyone waiting on a message.

What this buys the museum: a film whose every frame can be asked where does this come from, and answered.

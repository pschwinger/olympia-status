Kotinos - olive wreath, Athens 2004

The kotinos awarded to judoka Ilias Iliadis at Athens 2004 - the first Greek Olympic judo medal, won at SEVENTEEN. CORRECTED 2026-08-04: the Museum's own description says eighteen and we inherited it verbatim. Iliadis was born 10 November 1986 and competed in August 2004, so he was 17 years 9 months, and he is recorded as the youngest Olympic judo champion in history - a stronger claim than the Museum made, so this correction runs in their favour.

What a generated image must hold: This is a REAL MUSEUM ARTEFACT and its geometry is the one thing that must not change. Keep it exactly as in the reference image: DRIED golden-brown olive, never fresh or green; narrow pointed lanceolate leaves densely packed along the stems, not broad rounded leaves; small pale buds scattered through; thin dark wire ties binding the stems at intervals; a pale cloth strip wrapping the join; and an OPEN ring where the two ends overlap rather than a closed circle. Same shape, same leaves in the same positions, same wire ties, same cloth binding, same open ring.
Not asserted: This is NOT an ancient artefact. It is a 2004 revival of the ancient form. Nothing in frame should imply it was excavated or is of antiquity.
